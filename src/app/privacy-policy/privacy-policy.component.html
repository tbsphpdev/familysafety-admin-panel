Privacy Policy – ANGEL PROTECT

Last Updated: June 02, 2026

Introduction

Angel Protect App ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the Angel Protect App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Mobile Number
  • Date of Birth
  • Profile Information

Location Information

To provide family safety and location-sharing features, we collect:

  • Real-time location data
  • Location history
  • Safe zone entry and exit events

Location information is collected only when permission is granted by the user.

Device Information

We may collect:

  • Device Model
  • Operating System Version
  • App Version

Subscription Information

When purchasing a subscription, payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not store your payment card details.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide location-sharing services
  • Manage family groups
  • Send emergency SOS alerts
  • Manage subscriptions
  • Improve app performance and security
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers necessary to operate the application, including:

  • Firebase
  • Mapbox
  • SMS Service Providers (such as Twilio)
  • Cloud Hosting Providers

These providers receive only the minimum information necessary to perform their services.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

  • Passwords are encrypted and cannot be viewed by our staff.
  • Data is transmitted using secure encrypted connections.
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only.

Children's Privacy

Users under 18 years of age may be required to obtain parental or guardian consent before using certain features.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by applicable laws.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

Delete Account Within the App

Users can delete their account by:

  1. Opening the Angel Protect App.
  2. Navigating to Profile → Settings.
  3. Selecting "Delete Account".
  4. Confirming the deletion request.

Request Data Deletion

Users may also request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us at:

Email: contact@angelprotect-app.com

Subject Line: Account Deletion Request

Upon verification of the request, we will permanently delete the user's account and associated personal data within a reasonable timeframe, unless retention is required by law.

Data that may be deleted includes:

  • User Profile Information
  • Location History
  • Family Group Data
  • App Preferences

Some information may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory compliance purposes.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request a copy of your personal data

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the application and on our website.
